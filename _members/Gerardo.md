Gerardo studied Biology at the School of Sciences at UNAM in Mexico City. He then pursued his MSc and PhD at CINVESTAV in Irapuato, Mexico, under the supervision of Stewart Gillmor. His doctoral research was recognized with the 2017 Weizmann Prize from the Mexican Academy of Sciences. In 2018, he was awarded an EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowship to join the laboratory of Claudia Köhler at the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU). In 2021, he moved to Germany to continue his postdoctoral research at the Max Planck Institute of Molecular Plant Physiology. Throughout his scientific career, Gerardo has focused on understanding the molecular and epigenetic mechanisms underlying plant reproduction and seed development. In 2026, he joined the Institute of Biotechnology (IBt) at UNAM as a group leader, where he established the Laboratory of Genetic Plasticity and Developmental Genomics.
