# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-building-columns" %} About Cuernavaca

Cuernavaca is the capital of the state of Morelos, strategically located approximately 80 km south of Mexico City. Its privileged location allows residents to enjoy a peaceful, relaxed lifestyle while remaining within easy reach of one of Mexico’s most important cultural and economic centres.

## A Brief History

The name Cuernavaca derives from Cuauhnáhuac, a Nahuatl word commonly interpreted as "near the trees." The city’s origins date back to the 12th century, when it was established by the Tlahuica people. Thanks to its fertile lands and strategic location, the region later came under Aztec rule, becoming an important tributary centre renowned for its cotton production.

In 1521, the Spanish conquistador Hernán Cortés captured Cuernavaca. Drawn to its lush landscapes and favourable climate, Cortés subsequently established a residence there, constructing the imposing Palace of Cortés on the site of an earlier Indigenous ceremonial complex.

Cuernavaca is widely known as the "City of Eternal Spring," a nickname traditionally associated with the German naturalist and explorer Alexander von Humboldt, who visited the region in the early 19th century and admired its remarkably pleasant climate. In 1869, Cuernavaca officially became the capital of the newly established state of Morelos.

Throughout the 20th century and into the present day, Cuernavaca has continued to attract artists, intellectuals, diplomats, and travellers. Its year-round mild climate, beautiful natural surroundings, and proximity to Mexico City have established it as a desirable residential destination and a popular weekend retreat from the hustle and bustle of the capital.

Beyond its cultural and natural attractions, Cuernavaca is also an important center for scientific research and higher education. The city is home to UNAM's Morelos campus, which hosts several research institutes and centers specializing in metemathics, energy, genomics, physics and biotechnology, the [Institute of Biotechnology (IBt)]({{ '/about-ibt/' | relative_url }}), where our lab is located.
