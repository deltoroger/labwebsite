# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Research

Our research integrates genetics, epigenetics, and functional genomics to uncover the molecular mechanisms that govern plant development. We are particularly interested in the reproductive biology of flowering plants (angiosperms), focusing on how the three genetically distinct compartments of the seed—the embryo, endosperm, and maternal seed coat—communicate and coordinate their development to ensure successful seed formation. We also seek to understand how environmental stresses affect these developmental processes and ultimately influence plant reproductive success.
