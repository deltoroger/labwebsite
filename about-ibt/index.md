# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-building-columns" %} About the IBt

The [Institute of Biotechnology (IBt)](https://www.ibt.unam.mx/) is part of the [National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM)](https://www.unam.mx/) and is located on UNAM's Morelos campus in Cuernavaca, Mexico.

The IBt is a multidisciplinary research institute dedicated to advancing our understanding of biological systems through fundamental and applied research.

## A Brief History

The National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) is the largest higher education institution in Mexico and one of the leading research universities in Latin America. Its historical roots date back to 1551, with the establishment of the _Royal and Pontifical University of Mexico_. The modern university was established in 1910 and gained its autonomy in 1929.

Today, UNAM encompasses a broad academic and research network spanning the natural sciences, engineering, social sciences, humanities, and the arts. Its organizational structure includes 16 faculties, 36 research institutes, and numerous schools, research centers, and other academic units. Its historic main central campus in Mexico City was designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site, while its academic and research activities extend throughout Mexico, with major regional hubs beyond the capital.

The Institute of Biotechnology (IBt) was established in 1982 to strengthen the University's capabilities in the emerging fields of genetic engineering and biotechnology. Initially housed within the Institute of Biomedical Research in Mexico City, it relocated in 1985 to Cuernavaca, in the neighboring state of Morelos, as part of UNAM's broader efforts to decentralize scientific research. Since then, the IBt has become a major center for biological research and biotechnology in Mexico. The Institute is organized into five research departments, including the Department of Plant Molecular Biology, which is home to our research group.
