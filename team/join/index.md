# {% include icon.html icon="fa-regular fa-handshake" %} Join Us

We are always interested in welcoming curious and motivated researchers to our team! If you are passionate about plant biology, genetics, molecular biology, or bioinformatics, we would love to hear from you.

{% include section.html %}

## Postdoctoral Researchers

We welcome inquiries from motivated postdoctoral researchers interested in exploring the molecular and epigenetic mechanisms underlying plant reproduction and development.

Although funded positions may not always be available, we are happy to discuss research ideas and opportunities to apply for postdoctoral fellowships together.

{% include section.html %}

## Master's and PhD Students

We are looking for enthusiastic students interested in pursuing graduate research in plant molecular biology, genetics, epigenetics, and genomics.

Our laboratory combines experimental and computational approaches to investigate fundamental questions in plant reproduction and development.

We mainly recruit students through [the Master's and PhD Program in Biochemical Sciences]("https://bioquimicas.posgrado.unam.mx/") and the [PhD Program in Biomedical Sciencies]("https://pdcb.posgrado.unam.mx/index.php")

If you are interested in joining us, please get in touch to discuss potential projects and graduate opportunities.

{% include section.html %}

## Undergraduate Students

We also welcome motivated undergraduate students interested in gaining hands-on research experience through internships, Servicio Social, undergraduate theses, or research projects.

Previous research experience is not essential. Curiosity, enthusiasm, and a willingness to learn are what matter most!

{% include section.html %}
